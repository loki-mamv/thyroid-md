Thyroid Resources

Organizations, guidelines, and tools worth your time. Everything here comes from a medical society, a government health agency, or an established patient organization.

⚠️ Seek urgent care for these thyroid symptoms

• Chest pain, a very fast or irregular heartbeat, or fainting
• High fever with agitation or confusion in someone with known hyperthyroidism (possible thyroid storm)
• Severe drowsiness, confusion, or very low body temperature in someone with known hypothyroidism (possible myxedema coma)
• Sudden neck swelling with difficulty breathing or swallowing

Call 911 or go to the emergency department
